RESEARCH

Wii software as an information-preservation problem.

Future research will investigate Wii and WiiWare file structures, development remnants, software provenance, modification workflows, and preservation methodology.

The goal is not only to keep software runnable, but to preserve knowledge about how that software is structured, documented, identified, and understood.

01WiiWare research

Investigating individual titles, archives, file structures, and development artifacts.

02File structure analysis

Documenting unknown formats, binary structures, offsets, hashes, and relationships.

03LIS methodology

Applying metadata, provenance, classification, documentation, and preservation principles to reverse engineering.

ABOUT
