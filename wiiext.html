WHAT IT IS

A vocabulary layer for Wii game metadata.

PURPOSE

Consistent terminology

WiiExt provides controlled terminology for describing characteristics of Wii games, supporting consistent cataloguing, discovery, searching, filtering, and MARC 21 representation.

MARC 21

Field 655

MarcViiew records WiiExt terms using MARC 21 field 655, with $2 Wiiext identifying the source vocabulary.

THESAURUS

Relationships

Terms can carry types, source terms, broader terms, and narrower terms, allowing the vocabulary to support hierarchical navigation and discovery.

TERM TYPES

Describing software from several angles.

WiiExt currently covers terminology for genres, forms, themes, settings, and related characteristics. The vocabulary is intended to complement—not replace—the broader descriptive metadata used by MarcViiew.

01Genre

Gameplay types such as action, adventure, fighting, platform, puzzle, racing, role-playing, simulation, sports, strategy, and horror.

02Form

Software forms such as compilations, demos, samplers, educational software, multimedia software, emulators, and software.

03Theme & setting

Thematic and environmental concepts such as fantasy, science fiction, historic settings, and modern-day settings.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

A living vocabulary, documented like a library tool.

WiiExt is currently documented at version 1.0, while its broader/narrower hierarchy remains under development. Some relationships may be provisional, incomplete, or subject to revision as the vocabulary evolves.

The machine-readable vocabulary in the MarcViiew source code is the authoritative representation of the current term set. The profile documents its structure and intended use.

STATUS & SCOPE

Unofficial by design.

WiiExt is an independent vocabulary for MarcViiew. It is not an official Library of Congress vocabulary, extension, or standard, and is not endorsed, maintained, or administered by the Library of Congress.
