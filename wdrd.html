PROPOSED STANDARD · LIS × WII SOFTWARE

WDRD

Wii Digital Resource Description is a proposed metadata framework for describing WiiWare and Virtual Console resources, their technical structure, installed representations, and preservation evidence.

Documentation hubRead the research base
STATUSPLANNED

Targeted for the MarcViiew 0.9 BETA era alongside the planned UI update. No formal schema has been finalised yet.

WHY WDRD?

Describe the digital object behind the catalogue record.

MARC 21 gives MarcViiew a bibliographic encoding format, while RDA and related cataloguing principles provide ways to think about resources, attributes, and relationships. WDRD is intended to address the Wii-specific technical and preservation information that those general standards do not attempt to model.

IDENTIFICATION

What is this title?

Title IDs, title type, region, version, and other stable platform identifiers.

TECHNICAL

What does the platform say?

TMD information, content counts, boot indices, content types, sizes, hashes, and related title metadata.

REPRESENTATION

Where is it?

Observed NAND paths, title-local content, shared content, WAD representations, and distinctions between logical and physically observed content.

EVIDENCE

How certain are we?

Separate confirmed format knowledge, hardware observations, strong inferences, hypotheses, and unresolved questions.

INFLUENCES

Not a replacement for MARC or RDA.

WDRD would sit alongside existing standards rather than claiming to replace them. Its purpose is to create a Wii-specific descriptive layer that can feed or accompany conventional bibliographic records.

01MARC 21

Potential encoding/output layer for bibliographic records.

02RDA

Conceptual influence for entities, attributes, and relationships.

03AACR2

Historical cataloguing context and comparison point.

04Digital preservation

Evidence, provenance, technical metadata, and reproducibility.

PROPOSED MODEL

A layered description.

BIBLIOGRAPHIC

Resource identity

Title, creators, publisher, platform, release information, and other ordinary descriptive metadata.

WII PLATFORM

Title metadata

Title ID, TMD, ticket relationships, title version, required system information, and content inventory.

PHYSICAL / DIGITAL

Representation

Installed NAND representation, content objects, shared resources, WAD or SD representations, and observed paths.

PRESERVATION

Evidence

Source, observation method, confidence level, test conditions, unresolved questions, and research history.

ROADMAP

WDRD is deliberately not being finalised yet.

01Build the evidence base

Continue documenting NAND, TMD, content, WAD, WiiWare, and Virtual Console behaviour across real titles.

02Identify stable attributes

Separate platform facts from title-specific observations and avoid turning current hypotheses into schema requirements.

03Draft WDRD 0.1

Define fields, definitions, datatypes, required/optional status, provenance, evidence levels, and examples.

04Test through the UI

Use the MarcViiew 0.9 BETA interface to determine which WDRD information is actually useful to catalogue users.

STATUS

Planned, experimental, and intentionally open.

WDRD is a proposed project of the MarcViiew ecosystem. It is not an official Nintendo, MARC, RDA, AACR2, Library of Congress, or other standards-body specification.
